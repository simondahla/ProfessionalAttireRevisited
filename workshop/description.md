Adds 12 profession-themed clothing items to the Clothing Fabricator. Each one grants +2 to a
duplicant attribute while worn, so you can dress your dupes for the job they're actually doing.

Features

Craftable outfits, each boosting one attribute:

- Artist's outfit - Art
- Builder's outfit - Construction
- Cook's outfit - Cooking
- Digger's outfit - Digging
- Doctor's outfit - Caring
- Farmer's outfit - Botanist
- Rancher's outfit - Ranching
- Researcher's outfit - Learning
- Strongman's outfit - Strength
- Engineer's outfit - Machinery
- Pilot's outfit - Space Navigation (also speeds up rocket missions while worn)
- Hauler's outfit - Carry Amount

Fiber and ingredient costs are configurable per outfit through the in-game options menu (requires
PLib), so you can tune or disable any recipe without editing files.

Outfits currently reuse the vanilla Snazzy Suits sprite as a placeholder - the original mod's
unique art isn't included here, as it wasn't licensed for reuse. Real art is on the roadmap.
